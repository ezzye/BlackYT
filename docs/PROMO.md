# BlackYT educational Android promo

[MP4 film](https://github.com/ezzye/BlackYT/releases/download/v0.5.1/BlackYT-Promo-60s-Portrait.mp4) · [Portable offline HTML](https://github.com/ezzye/BlackYT/releases/download/v0.5.1/BlackYT-promo.html) · [English captions](BlackYT-promo-captions.srt)

The 60-second, eight-scene portrait film introduces music and creator search, spoken-video discovery, Library saves and resume, editable Explore topics, manual queue and artwork swipe, direct YT audio streaming and screen-off listening. It uses actual native BlackYT v0.5.1 UI recordings and animation. It does not imply iOS availability, offline audio downloads, cloud Library sync, podcast RSS feeds, automatic subscriptions or recommendations.

The app demonstration was captured from a physical Pixel. Real waits were cut and final frames held for readability; UI video keeps native speed. The Explore sequence shows editing and Save, then Home; its edited Jazz tile is below the initial fold. Pocket and screen-off visuals are illustrations following real player footage, not fabricated lock-screen evidence. Playback/device verification remains in the separately versioned app reports.

Public example episode metadata and artwork (including Wes Roth and Errol Elliott) remain the property of their respective rights holders. A staged Library contains the public example episode and creator. The owner’s app preferences were restored after recording. No device status bar, private Library or listening history, unrelated app content, raw phone captures or third-party song/episode audio is published in these promo assets.

Narration: ElevenLabs Jamie - Upbeat British Ad Voice, Eleven v4, synthetic stock voice, no human voice cloning. Applicable account/service terms govern the generated voice; this credit does not claim broader rights. Music and short effects were synthesized procedurally for the film without recorded song samples. Mascot: BlackYT project artwork. Typography: Inter and Space Grotesk (SIL Open Font License). Animation: GSAP and HyperFrames. No affiliation with YT or Spotify is implied.

The owner approved the storyboard and revised ElevenLabs preview before export. The delivery is H.264/AAC stereo, 1080×1920, 30 fps, with burned-in English captions; a separate SRT is provided. All 1,800 video frames decode without reported errors. The actual encoded output was inspected across all eight scenes; final loudness is about −15.8 LUFS with −2.5 dBFS true peak. These are technical checks, separate from playback acceptance on every device.
