A practical guide · Android

Find something. Make it yours.

BlackYT streams music and videos from YT. These steps cover search, queues, playlists and the phone’s Library.

01

Play now or add it next

  1. Open Search and choose Music for songs or All videos for talks, interviews and other spoken videos.
  2. Search for a song, artist, show, guest or topic, then tap a result to play it now.
  3. To keep listening to the current item, choose Add on a result. It goes to the end of your queue. Use the mini player or Now Playing for pause, seek, next, previous and queue controls.

For a channel, choose Creators, find it, then browse its Videos. BlackYT does not automatically identify podcasts.

02

Build and play a playlist

  1. In Search, select one or more results and choose Create playlist. Give it a name. Or choose Add to playlist to use one you have already made.
  2. Open Library to find your saved playlists.
  3. Play playlist replaces the current queue and starts the playlist. Add all places its items after the current queue and keeps current playback going.
03

Return to an episode or creator

  1. Save an episode or creator to Library. A saved episode keeps its listening progress; opening a saved creator fetches its current videos.
  2. Tap a saved episode to resume. Saved creators are under Library → Saved creators.
  3. Share a supported YT video link from another app to BlackYT, or paste it into Search. Check the preview, then tap the result to play. A supported short link can retain its starting time.

Saved details and progress stay on this phone. They do not sync to an account or cloud.

04

Shape Explore around your interests

  1. Open Home → Explore, then choose Edit.
  2. Add a topic and the search phrase you want it to use. You can edit, move or remove topics later.
  3. For talks and episodes, leave Music results only off. Choose All videos when searching spoken content.
05

Ask for optional suggestions

Choose Find more like these from a playlist when you want ideas for similar videos. It checks up to two creator pages and two searches, and can return up to 12 suggestions.

Suggestions are never added automatically. “New to this playlist” does not verify when a video was uploaded.

A little preparation

Continue listening

Playlist autoplay: Tap any song to keep listening through the playlist. Search results and episode lists continue in the same way. Offline, BlackYT skips to the next fully cached item in your queue. Pause and Stop stay in your control.

Before going underground

Get ready on Wi-Fi while you still have a connection. Journey buffer can help through short gaps in signal.

  1. Connect to Wi-Fi and open Library, then choose your playlist.
  2. Tap Play playlist to start it. Leave BlackYT playing and give the audio time to arrive.
  3. Look for a small green ✓ beside each item you want ready. Then lock your screen as usual.

✓ Ready offline means the whole audio file is saved and has not expired. ◐ Part saved means only some is saved; ○ Needs connection means saved playable audio cannot currently be established; … Checking means the app is checking. Long-press an icon for the full explanation and, when known, the percentage saved. Screen readers also announce when a ready item expires. Icons refresh about every two seconds while visible and when you return to the app.

The full player shows the written saved-audio status and the seconds buffered ahead separately. Buffered seconds alone do not mean the whole item is ready.

Preparation covers the current item and up to the next two in the queue. The next items can each save up to about 20 minutes, capped at 24 MiB each; the total temporary cache is 256 MiB. Audio expires six hours after it is first saved; listening again does not extend that time. Android can clear it sooner. This helps with short outages. It does not automatically save an entire playlist or library; unprepared audio still needs a connection.

Use the top-right menu → Journey buffer to check settings. Preparation defaults to unmetered connections; choosing Wi-Fi and mobile data may use your data allowance. Clear saved audio and Off remove saved audio without deleting playlist details.

Plain about privacy

Your Library stays on your phone.

No BlackYT account is needed, and there is no BlackYT cloud sync. Playlists, saved videos and creators, listening progress, recent searches and preferences are stored on your phone. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove them.

Search, artwork and audio requests go directly to YT/Google. They can receive your device’s IP address, your searches, requested video or channel details, and request information. BlackYT is not an anonymity service.

The inspected app build has no in-app ad player or analytics SDK. Creator sponsorships may remain, and upstream services can change. An ad-free experience is not guaranteed.

On this website, no advertising or analytics script is added. The web host still receives ordinary connection information, and GitHub receives download requests. The promo plays from this site without a third-party embedded player.

FAQ & troubleshooting

Answers for the small snags.

Email the project

Which phones can use BlackYT?

Android phones and tablets running Android 8.0 or newer. BlackYT is not currently available for iPhone or iPad. An iOS app is planned for later, with no release date.

Do I need Developer options, USB debugging or Termux?

No. Normal installation uses the APK on your Android device. You do not need a computer, Developer options, USB debugging, Termux, a Mac or a custom certificate.

Android says my browser can’t install apps from this source.

Allow installation for only the app that opened the APK, then return to Downloads and open the APK again. On Pixel, the route is Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps; choose Chrome or Files, whichever opened the APK. You can revoke the permission after installation. Menu names vary by phone.

I see a Play Protect, work or school warning.

Stop the installation. Do not turn off Play Protect or bypass the warning. Contact blackyt@streamit2.me or your device administrator.

The app will not install or says there is a signature conflict.

Check that your phone runs Android 8.0 or newer and that the download finished. Avoid uninstalling the current app or clearing its storage to experiment. Send the exact error and phone model to blackyt@streamit2.me.

A playlist stops at the start, even though it says Ready offline.

Update to the latest release first: download the Android app, open the APK and choose Update over your existing app. Do not uninstall, so your saved playlists stay in place. The update can refresh a conflicting temporary buffer automatically once. If it still stops, open track options and choose Retry current song, then check your connection. As a last resort, Journey buffer → Clear saved audio clears the temporary six-hour cache, not your saved playlists. Ready offline describes the temporary saved audio; it cannot guarantee indefinite service availability.

A song starts but Bluetooth is silent.

Check the connected audio output and media volume on both your phone and Bluetooth device. Try play/pause or another song. The buffer-format issue fixed in rc4 has not been proven to be caused by Bluetooth.

Search is empty or a result will not play.

Check your connection and try a shorter search or All videos. Some videos may be unavailable or restricted, and YT can change its service. If playback stops with an error, use Retry current song or try another result.

How do I find spoken videos?

Choose Search → All videos, or search in Creators and open a channel’s Videos. Try a show, guest, speaker or topic name. BlackYT does not automatically label videos as podcasts.

Will music keep playing with my screen locked?

Background playback depends on Android and your phone manufacturer. Reopen BlackYT and resume if playback stops. If available, check BlackYT’s app-specific battery setting. On Pixel, open Settings → Apps → BlackYT → App battery usage and check Allow background usage. Menu labels can vary. General battery protections do not need to be switched off.

Can I listen offline? Does BlackYT guarantee no ads?

Audio marked with the green check is currently ready to play from the temporary cache. Prepare on Wi-Fi before you lose signal. The buffer is limited and expires; it is not a permanent download library. Creator sponsorships can remain and an ad-free experience is not guaranteed.

Where are playlists and saved episodes kept?

On this phone. There is no BlackYT account or cloud sync. Uninstalling or clearing app storage may remove saved playlists, videos, creators and progress.
