BlackYT Phone

Search, filter and listen on this phone. Termux fetches YT audio on this phone and feeds the local player.

Back to player

Start listening

Open Termux, type blackyt, then press Enter. Keep Termux running and open BlackYT Phone. After a phone restart, start Termux again.

The phone player lives at http://127.0.0.1:8765/. The older app with a 192.168 address needs the Mac. Use the new BlackYT Phone icon.

Wi-Fi and mobile data

If a song stops after changing networks, tap Retry track for a fresh YT link. If searching fails, check mobile data and confirm Termux is running.

No cloud server is used. Normal mobile data and battery usage apply. A VPN should use the same connection for Termux and Chrome; excluding only one can prevent playback.

Screen off

Keep Termux's session running while listening. Allow background activity for Termux in Android app information if needed. The launcher holds a wake lock until you stop it; Android can still terminate a background app.

To stop the helper, return to its Termux session and press Ctrl + C. Restart with blackyt.

Filters and playback

Music checks YT's Music category when you play. Interests uses your include and exclude phrases in titles and channel names. Include matches any phrase; exclude wins. Add builds a queue; Play starts a result.

Search results and direct stream availability depend on YT. This unofficial route can change or be blocked. Messages inside the creator's recording remain part of the audio. Offline mode saves the interface, not songs.

First phone test

  1. Turn off Wi-Fi and use mobile data.
  2. Make a new search and play two queued tracks.
  3. Make the Mac unavailable, lock the phone and listen for two minutes and through a track change.

Earlier successful tests used the Mac-assisted version. This Termux version needs its own phone test.
