The episode was playing a dubbed audio track.

Cause confirmed: BlackYT chose the first compatible audio stream, which for this Wes Roth episode was Arabic. The same video also provides the original English speech. Version 0.5.1 now prefers the creator’s original audio when YT identifies it.

The checked video is “AI just BROKE all of the software industry…” by Wes Roth, video ID EmaXeEdlJkI. Both audio tracks were supplied successfully by YT. This particular problem was audio selection.

Method: short private excerpts, local multilingual speech recognition, and comparison with your supplied transcript. Recognition can miss names; these checks cover sampled sections, rather than every word of the 26-minute episode. Audio and the full transcript stay out of GitHub.

What changed

The app now prefers a supported original track, then an unlabelled track, then another available alternative. It prefers MP4 within each group and keeps WebM as a fallback. It does not force English: an original French, Arabic or other-language upload remains in its creator’s original language. A manual language selector is not included.

Phone verification

Verified on the connected Pixel: 120.021 seconds of wall time advanced audio by 119.866 seconds, at speed 1.0 and pitch 1.0. The phone stayed locked with the display off throughout. After seeking near the end of the episode, the queue advanced automatically to a music track while still locked. This test was muted within BlackYT; it does not replace your listening check. 26 release unit tests and 5 focused phone tests passed; lint found no errors.

Use the update

Update BlackYT over the existing app, then select the episode again. There is no need to uninstall it or remove saved episodes. Normal installation does not require developer settings, Termux, a certificate, a Mac or USB.
